about Slipzy

many people lose paper receipts, forget warranty expiry dates, miss return deadlines and find it hard to track their spending. Slipzy keeps all your purchase information organized, searchable and safely stored online. each user can only see their own records.

one private account for every registered user
reminders handled by the system itself
pdf reports of purchases and expenses

features

add purchases

a simple form for store, date, products, quantities, prices and total.

track warranties

expiry is calculated for you and shown as active or expired.

return deadlines

add a return date for each product so nothing is missed.

search and filter

find purchases by store, product, date or category. view, edit or delete.

spending totals

monthly, overall and per-category spending, worked out for you.

pdf reports

generate and download reports of your purchases and expenses.
